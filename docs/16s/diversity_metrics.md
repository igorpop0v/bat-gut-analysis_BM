# Selection of 16S rRNA diversity metrics

## Study groups

The analysis includes three biological groups:

- active before hibernation;
- hibernation;
- active after hibernation.

The positive-control sample was excluded before diversity analysis.

## Rarefaction depth

The feature table was rarefied to 18,000 sequences per sample. This depth
retained all 76 biological samples.

## Primary alpha-diversity metrics

The primary alpha-diversity metrics used for statistical analysis and
visualization are:

- Shannon diversity, representing both richness and evenness;
- Simpson diversity, emphasizing the contribution of dominant features;
- Pielou's evenness, representing the evenness component of diversity.

Observed features were retained as a supporting rarefaction result but were
not selected as a primary outcome because the richness rarefaction curves did
not reach a clear plateau at the selected sequencing depth.

## Primary beta-diversity metric

Bray-Curtis dissimilarity was selected as the primary beta-diversity metric
because the analysis focuses on abundance-weighted differences in microbial
community composition.

## Additional QIIME 2 outputs

Jaccard, Faith's phylogenetic diversity, and weighted and unweighted UniFrac
results are generated by the standard QIIME 2 core-metrics workflow and are
retained as exploratory outputs. They are not included in the primary
statistical analysis or the main R figures.

The primary metrics follow the previously used analysis plan and were selected
based on their ecological interpretation rather than statistical significance.
